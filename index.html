FAQs

Every partnership starts with a conversation, not a term sheet. We take the time to understand your business, your goals, and what a good outcome looks like for you before we talk structure.

We work across majority and minority positions, growth equity, and private credit, structured around what makes sense for the business rather than a one-size-fits-all template.

Valuation depends on growth trajectory, margins, and market position. We'll walk you through our thinking directly and transparently rather than leaving you guessing.

Most founders stay on and keep running the business day to day. We're there for strategic support and capital, not to replace the people who built it.

Your team is part of what we're investing in. We back businesses with the intent to grow them, and that means growing the people already there.

In most cases your brand stays exactly as it is. We back what's already working rather than folding it into something else.

We concentrate on growth equity, private credit, and real assets, with a preference for founder-led businesses with proven fundamentals.

It's how we work: alongside founders, for the long term, not just as a source of capital that shows up once a quarter.
